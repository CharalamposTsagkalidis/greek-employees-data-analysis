# Survey on Work Environment and Management Practices in Greece

This repository contains the research data and statistical analysis for my **MBA Thesis in Information Systems** at the **University of Western Macedonia**. 

The study explores the quality of the work environment, organizational structures, and management practices within the Greek technology and consulting sectors.

## 📋 Project Overview
The primary goal of this research is to evaluate how management practices influence the professional lives of technology experts in Greece. 

* **Target Population:** Professionals in Technology and Consulting companies.
* **Sample Size:** 48 respondents (primarily developers with 1–3 years of experience).
* **Data Format:** Anonymized Excel file (.xlsx) containing no sensitive or personally identifiable information.

## 📊 Statistical Analysis
The analysis was performed using **R**. The following statistical methods and tests were utilized:

* **Internal Consistency:** Cronbach’s Alpha to ensure survey reliability.
* **Distribution Analysis:** Normality checks and Boxplots to visualize data spread and outliers.

* **Hypothesis Testing:** T-tests to compare means between different groups.
* **Predictive Modeling:** Linear regression to identify correlations between management variables and environment quality.


## 🛠️ Requirements
To replicate the analysis, you will need:
* [R](https://www.r-project.org/) (version 4.0 or higher)
* Key libraries: `ggplot2`, `psych`, `car`, `readxl`
